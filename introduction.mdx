## Overview

AC provides a centralized space for your project documentation. You can create, organize, and collaborate on docs effortlessly. It supports markdown, rich media, and integrates with your workflows to keep everything up-to-date.

This platform scales from small projects to enterprise teams, offering search, versioning, and sharing features out of the box.

## Benefits for Teams and Projects

AC streamlines your documentation process, reducing onboarding time for new members by centralizing knowledge. Teams benefit from reduced context-switching and improved consistency.

You gain audit trails for compliance, easy exports for backups, and integrations with tools like GitHub or Slack.

<Expandable title="Advanced Benefits" default-open="false">

- **Scalability**: Handles thousands of pages without performance issues.
- **Security**: Role-based access control and private spaces.
- **Customization**: Themes, custom domains, and API access.

</Expandable>

## Quick Start

Get up and running in minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Account" icon="user-plus">
    Sign up at `https://app.ac.com` with your email or SSO provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="New Space" icon="plus">
    Click "New Space" and name it for your project, like "Project Docs".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add First Doc" icon="file-plus">
    Create a page with markdown. Use headings, lists, and code blocks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Team" icon="share-2">
    Share the space link and set permissions for collaborators.
  </Step>
</Steps>
