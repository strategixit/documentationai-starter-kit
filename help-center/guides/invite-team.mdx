## Overview

Easily collaborate by inviting your team to your AC project.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your project">
    Go to your AC dashboard and select your project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Team tab">
    Click the "Team" tab in your project sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add teammates">
    Enter your teammate's email and click "Invite". They will receive an email to join your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>
