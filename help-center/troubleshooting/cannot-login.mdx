---
title: Cannot log in to AC
description: Simple steps if you can't access your AC account.
---

## Symptoms

- Login form rejects password
- "Incorrect password" or "Account not found"

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reset your password">
    Go to the login page, click "Forgot password?", and follow the instructions in your email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    Ensure you are using the email address you registered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    If problems persist, email support@ac.com for assistance.
  </Step>
</Steps>
