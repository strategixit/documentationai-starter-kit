## Overview

Creating your first project in AC is easy.

1. Log in to your AC dashboard
2. Click the "+ New Project" button on the top right
3. Enter your project name and optional description
4. Click "Create"
5. Your project is ready to organize documentation

You can now add docs, invite teammates, and manage content from your project home.
