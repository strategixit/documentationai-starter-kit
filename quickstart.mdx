## Prerequisites

<Callout kind="info" title="Before You Begin">
Ensure you have:
- A valid email address for account registration
- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- Optional: A GitHub account for version control integration
</Callout>

## Step 1: Create Your AC Account

Follow these steps to register and log in to AC.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the AC Dashboard" icon="monitor">
    Open your browser and navigate to `https://dashboard.example.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign Up" icon="user-plus">
    Click the **Sign Up** button. Fill in your email, password, and display name. Accept the terms of service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from AC. Click the link to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Return to the dashboard and enter your credentials to access the main interface.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Create Your First Documentation Space

A documentation space organizes your project docs. Set one up now.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Spaces" icon="folder">
    From the dashboard sidebar, select **Documentation Spaces**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create New Space" icon="plus">
    Click **New Space**. Enter a name like `My Project Docs` and select a template (e.g., "Software").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Settings" icon="settings">
    Set visibility to **Private** or **Public**, and add a description: "Documentation for My Project".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Enter" icon="check-circle">
    Click **Create**. You'll land in your new space's overview page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Upload Your Initial Documents

Add content to your space.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a New Page" icon="file-plus">
    In your space, click **New Page**. Name it `README.md`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Content" icon="type">
    Paste initial markdown:

````markdown
# Welcome to My Project

This is your starting documentation.
````

  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Files" icon="upload">
    Drag and drop images or docs into the editor, or use **Insert > File**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish" icon="globe">
    Click **Publish** to make it live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip" title="Pro Tip">
Customize your space theme with brand color `#1F883D` in **Settings > Appearance**.
</Callout>
